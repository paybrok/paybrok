# Remittances

Paybrok remittance flows are designed for users who want to send value across countries using digital dollars and local delivery providers.

## Flow

1. The user chooses origin, destination, amount, asset, and delivery method.
2. Paybrok shows available bridge/provider options where available.
3. The user selects a provider.
4. Funds are locked in escrow.
5. The provider delivers locally.
6. Funds are released after delivery confirmation.
7. If delivery fails, a dispute can be opened.

## Examples

- Sending digital dollars to someone who needs local currency.
- Paying a recipient through a local bank, wallet, or payment method.
- Coordinating delivery with approved providers and a recorded payment process.

## Availability and Operating Conditions

- Availability depends on country, route, provider liquidity, payment method, compliance review, and operational limits.
- Confirm the active provider, delivery method and quote for your route in the app before protecting funds.

# What Is Paybrok?

Paybrok is a non-custodial wallet and protected payment platform built around real payment needs.

It helps users hold and move digital dollars, collect payments with QR or links, coordinate local P2P payments, protect service payments with escrow, and work with approved providers for local delivery or remittance-style flows.

## The Simple Idea

Paybrok brings wallet control and payment coordination into one experience.

A user can:

- Create or restore a wallet.
- Receive digital dollars.
- Send funds.
- Charge with QR or payment links.
- Scan QR codes.
- Use local providers where available.
- Protect P2P or service payments with escrow.
- Open a dispute when an order goes wrong.

## Self-Custody

Paybrok is designed as a non-custodial product. Users control their wallet credentials. Paybrok does not store a user's seed phrase on Paybrok servers.

This matters because users can use payment tools without handing over full custody of their wallet.

## Practical Payments

Paybrok is not only a wallet screen. It is designed for practical situations:

- A merchant wants to collect with a QR.
- A freelancer wants payment protection.
- A client wants to avoid paying everything upfront.
- A user wants to pay a local QR using digital dollars.
- A person wants to send value across a route with a local provider.
- A P2P buyer and seller need structure, escrow, and dispute tools.

## Platform

Paybrok supports web and mobile experiences. Availability of specific features can vary by platform, country, provider liquidity, compliance review, and configuration.

## Freighter

Freighter can be useful for advanced Stellar users on web. Paybrok may support Freighter where appropriate, but Paybrok also has its own wallet and payment flows.

# Paybrok Providers

Providers are approved users or businesses that help deliver local liquidity, local payments, or service execution.

## Provider Types

- Local liquidity providers.
- Remittance bridge providers.
- Service providers.
- Merchants or businesses receiving local QR payments.

## What Providers Can Do

Depending on approval and configuration, providers may:

- Publish available liquidity.
- Accept P2P orders.
- Fulfill local payments.
- Support remittance delivery.
- Receive protected service payments.
- Build reputation through completed orders.

## Provider Review

Paybrok may review provider information such as:

- Identity or business information.
- Country and payment methods.
- Operational capacity.
- Contact information.
- Dispute history.
- Risk indicators.

## Provider Safety

Providers should never ask users for:

- Seed phrases.
- Private keys.
- Web wallet passwords.
- PINs.
- Unrelated personal data.

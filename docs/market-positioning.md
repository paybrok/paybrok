# Market Positioning

Paybrok is positioned as a more complete native non-custodial wallet and payment app for Stellar-based digital payments across Android, iOS, and web.

The product combines several layers that are often separated across different financial and crypto products:

- Native wallet experience.
- Non-custodial Stellar payments.
- QR payment requests.
- Payment links.
- QR scanning.
- Wallet movement history.
- P2P flows.
- Escrow and dispute support.
- Reputation and score features.
- Rewards and ambassador-oriented growth loops.
- Unified Flutter codebase across Android, iOS, and web.

## Product Thesis

Paybrok is built around a simple idea:

> Digital payments should feel as simple as a regular payment app while preserving the self-custody benefits of a wallet.

Many crypto wallets are powerful but difficult for everyday users. Many simple money apps are easy to use but custodial. Paybrok is designed to sit between those worlds: a practical payment experience with a native non-custodial wallet model.

Paybrok's advantage is not a single isolated feature. Its advantage is the combination of wallet, payments, QR/link requests, P2P, escrow, disputes, reputation, rewards, and multi-platform Flutter delivery in one product.

Paybrok challenges the idea that users must choose between a simple custodial app and a powerful but complex crypto wallet. It is built to raise the standard for practical non-custodial payments in Latin America and beyond.

## Comparable Product Categories

Paybrok touches several product categories:

- Stellar wallets.
- Digital dollar apps.
- QR payment apps.
- P2P exchange flows.
- Reputation-based marketplace tools.
- Payment-link products.
- Rewards and ambassador programs.

Paybrok does not aim to be only one of these categories. Its positioning is the superior integration of wallet, payments, P2P, reputation, and reward-oriented activity into a single product experience.

Instead of forcing users to jump between multiple apps for wallet control, payment requests, P2P coordination, trust signals, and rewards, Paybrok brings these layers together in one native product.

## Differentiators

### Native Wallet

Paybrok has its own native wallet experience. It is not defined by Freighter or any external browser wallet. Freighter exists and can be useful for advanced users or selected web flows, but Paybrok has its own Android, iOS, and web wallet/payment experience.

### Non-Custodial Model

Paybrok is designed as a non-custodial wallet and payment app. Users control their wallet. Paybrok focuses on payment usability without presenting itself as a bank or custodian.

### Stellar-Based Payments

Paybrok is built around Stellar-compatible payment flows. Stellar provides fast and low-cost payment infrastructure for supported assets and wallet movement flows.

### QR And Payment Links

Paybrok supports QR and link-based payment requests. This makes it useful for practical everyday scenarios where one user wants to request a payment and another user needs to scan or open a link with details already prepared.

### P2P With Escrow And Disputes

Paybrok includes P2P flows with escrow and dispute support. This gives the product a broader role than a basic wallet because it can support payment coordination between users.

### Reputation And Score

Paybrok includes reputation and score features. This is important for P2P activity because trust signals can help users evaluate counterparties and activity history.

### Rewards

Paybrok includes reward-oriented product features tied to real wallet and payment activity. Rewards, score, and ambassador flows are designed to support user growth and retention.

### Unified Flutter Codebase

Paybrok is built in Flutter for Android, iOS, and web. A unified codebase allows the product to move faster across platforms and maintain a consistent experience.

## Market Summary

Paybrok combines elements commonly found in separate products:

- A Stellar wallet.
- A simple digital payment app.
- QR and payment-link tools.
- P2P marketplace flows.
- Escrow and dispute support.
- Reputation and rewards.

This combination is Paybrok's main market position: a more complete native payment wallet for users who need wallet control, simple payment UX, QR/link payments, P2P functionality, reputation signals, and rewards in one product.

In practical terms, Paybrok is designed to offer a stronger integrated experience than products that only solve one layer: only wallet storage, only QR payments, only custodial digital dollars, or only P2P matching.

The challenge Paybrok takes on is direct: make non-custodial payments feel ready for everyday use, while keeping the product broad enough to support real P2P behavior, trust, rewards, and growth loops.

## Current Challenges

Paybrok is a growing product. Its main challenges are not only technical; they also include:

- User adoption.
- P2P liquidity.
- Public trust.
- Regulatory clarity by market.
- App store distribution and platform rollout.
- Continued education around non-custodial payments.

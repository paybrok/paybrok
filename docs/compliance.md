# Compliance and Legal Position

This document is a public summary and is not legal advice.

## Non-Custodial Model

Paybrok is designed as a non-custodial wallet and protected payment platform. In the base wallet model, users control their wallet credentials and assets on the Stellar network.

## AML/CTF Approach

Paybrok applies a risk-based approach to anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing controls.

Controls may include:

- Provider review.
- Transaction and order monitoring.
- Sanctions screening.
- Limits or restrictions based on risk.
- Dispute documentation.
- Suspension of suspicious activity.
- Cooperation with lawful requests.

## Public Policies

Paybrok maintains public-facing legal and compliance materials such as:

- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- AML/CTF Policy.
- Arbitration notice.

## Third-Party Providers

Where Paybrok integrates with third-party providers, those providers may have their own:

- KYC requirements.
- Fees.
- Country restrictions.
- Compliance obligations.
- Availability limits.

Paybrok does not store card data or replace third-party compliance requirements.

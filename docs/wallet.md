# Paybrok Wallet

Paybrok is designed around self-custody. Users control their wallet credentials, and Paybrok does not store the user's seed phrase on Paybrok servers.

## Mobile Wallet

On mobile, Paybrok provides a wallet experience for:

- Creating a wallet.
- Restoring a wallet.
- Sending assets.
- Receiving assets.
- Scanning QR codes.
- Viewing balances.
- Viewing movements.
- Using Paybrok payment links and QR codes.

## Web Wallet

On web, Paybrok supports:

- Paybrok web wallet with local encrypted vault.
- Local password for unlocking sensitive wallet actions.
- Recovery with seed/recovery words if local browser storage is lost.
- Sign locally with the Paybrok wallet.

## Prepare an Asset Before Receiving

Open Prepare wallet, choose the asset and wait until it is ready. When available, sponsored preparation covers the required account and asset reserves; it does not add spendable XLM. Only the selected asset is prepared. Use the same asset and Stellar network at both ends of a transfer.

## Recovery

Users must keep their recovery words safe.

Paybrok will never ask for recovery words through:

- Telegram.
- Email.
- Support chat.
- Phone call.
- Social media.
- Provider message.

If you can still access the wallet on another device, open Backup and save the recovery words after authenticating locally. If both your backup and access to all devices are lost, Paybrok cannot reconstruct the keys.

## Supported Assets

Supported assets may vary by platform and configuration. Paybrok is focused on Stellar-based assets such as:

- USDC.
- EURC.
- XLM.
- BROK where enabled.
